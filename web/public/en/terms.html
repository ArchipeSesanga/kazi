1. Who we are

Kazi is a free Android app published by Archipe Sesanga (individual developer), based in South Africa (“Kazi”, “we”). It lets clients find local service providers in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Kinshasa first) and contact them by WhatsApp or phone.

2. Our role

Kazi connects people. We are not a party to any agreement between a client and a provider, we handle no payments, and we do not guarantee the quality, price or availability of any service. If a “verified” badge is shown on a profile, it means an identity check was done; it is not a guarantee of the provider’s work.

  • You give accurate information and are responsible for how your account is used.
  • One account per person.

4. Providers

  • A listing title must not contain a phone number. The number of active listings per provider is limited.
  • Your profile may be approved, rejected or suspended by our moderation team.

5. Prohibited content and behaviour

Scams and fake listings, impersonation, illegal, violent, discriminatory or sexual content, harassment, spam, fake reviews, collecting other users’ data, and any attempt to bypass the service’s security.

6. Reports and moderation

Any signed-in user can report a listing or a profile. When content has three open reports from different users it is hidden automatically pending review by a moderator, who can restore it, hide it or suspend the provider. We may also hide a listing whose title contains a phone number.
