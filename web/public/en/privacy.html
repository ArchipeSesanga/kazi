2. Basis and purposes

Providing the service you ask for, your consent (public provider number, notifications, identity verification), and our legitimate interest in protecting the service against fraud and abuse.

3. Sharing and processors

We do not sell your data. We use Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, notifications, analytics, crash reports) as a processor; database and storage data are hosted in the South Africa region (africa-south1). Sign-in SMS messages are sent through Firebase Authentication.

4. Security

Sign-in by SMS code, strict access rules (each user reads only their own data), identity documents in a private area only moderators can open, and a log of moderation actions.

5. Your rights

  • Access and correction: edit your profile in the app.
  • Deletion: in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or through the Delete my account page. Your listings, profile, favourites, contacts and photos are deleted; reviews you wrote stay visible without your name.
  • Questions or complaints: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} · WhatsApp {{SUPPORT_WHATSAPP}}.

6. Minors

Kazi is for people aged 18 and over.
