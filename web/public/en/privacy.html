2. Why we use it

To provide the service you ask for (account, profile, listings, connecting clients and providers), to keep it safe (reports, blocks, abuse detection), and to understand and improve the app (usage, crashes). We do not sell your data and we show no advertising.

3. Service providers and where data is stored

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC and its affiliates) as a processor for:

  • Firebase Authentication: phone sign-in, including sending the SMS code;
  • Cloud Firestore: profiles, listings, contacts, reports, blocks, favourites. Region: africa-south1 (Johannesburg, South Africa);
  • Cloud Storage for Firebase: photos. Region: {{STORAGE_REGION}};
  • Cloud Functions: moderation rules, account deletion, limits. Region: africa-south1. Two scheduled clean-up tasks (old draft listings, orphan photos) run in the europe-west1 (Belgium) region and handle listing and photo data;
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check (Play Integrity) and Remote Config: global Google services; their data may be processed in the countries where Google operates them;
  • Firebase Hosting: this website.

When you tap WhatsApp or Call you leave Kazi: the conversation happens in WhatsApp or by phone, is not recorded by Kazi, and WhatsApp’s or your carrier’s policy applies. Kazi moderators access the data needed for moderation through an admin app protected by a password and by roles reserved for the team.

4. How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (in the app, or on request, see Delete my account):

Deleted:

  • your sign-in account (phone number) and your profile;
  • your favourites, blocks and registered devices;
  • your provider profile, all your listings and their photos, and any verification documents;
  • your contact records (those where you are the client and those where you are the provider) and favourites other users had saved about you;
  • your stored photos and files (your account’s folder);
  • the report counter tied to your account.

Anonymised and kept:

  • reviews you wrote: your name and photo are removed and the review is detached from your id; the rating and text stay visible and count in the provider’s rating;
  • reports you filed: kept as a safety record, with the “reporter” field replaced by “deleted”.

Kept: the log of moderation decisions (ids and the decision, for example “listing hidden”), which serves as an audit trail; usage and crash data, which is not linked to your identity, is kept for the retention period we have configured for Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics in the Firebase console.

Deletion is permanent. If a deletion is interrupted, it can safely be run again.

5. Your rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and edit your profile in the app. To get a copy of the data we hold about you, write to us.
  • Deletion: in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or on request by email or WhatsApp, see Delete my account.
  • Withdrawing consent: to stop your numbers being visible, delete your account (deleting the provider profile alone is not available yet).
  • Questions or complaints: moisemozess@gmail.com · WhatsApp +27 62 129 7910.

6. Minimum age

Kazi is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors; if you believe a minor has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Security

  • Traffic between the app and our Firebase services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS); Google encrypts stored data by default.
  • Access rules limit every read and write: nothing is readable without signing in, and your private data can only be read by you and moderators.
  • The app uses Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) to limit use by modified apps.
  • Reports are rate-limited per user per day, and moderation actions are recorded in a log.

No system is completely secure. Keep your phone and SMS codes to yourself.
